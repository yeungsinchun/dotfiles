HKDSE Physics General Marking Instruction

1. It is very important that all markers should adhere as closely as possible to the marking scheme. In many cases, however, candidates may have obtained a correct answer by an alternative method not specified in the marking scheme. In general, a correct answer merits the answer mark allocated to that part, unless a particular method has been specified in the question. Markers should be patient in marking alternative solutions not specified in the marking scheme.

2. In the marking scheme, answer marks or ‘A’ marks are awarded for a correct numerical answer with a unit. In case the same unit involved is given incorrectly for more than once in the same question, the ‘A’ marks thereafter can be awarded even for correct numerical answers without units. If the answer should be in km, then cm and m are considered to be wrong units.

3. In a question consisting of several parts each depending on the previous parts, marks for correct method or substitution are awarded to steps or methods correctly deduced from previous answers, even if these answers are erroneous or for inserting values of appropriate physical quantities into an algebraic expression irrespective of their order of magnitudes. However, ‘A’ marks for the corresponding answers should NOT be awarded (unless otherwise specified).

4. For the convenience of markers, the marking scheme is written as detailed as possible. However, it is still likely that candidates would not present their solution in the same explicit manner, e.g. some steps would either be omitted or stated implicitly. In such cases, markers should exercise their discretion in marking candidates’ work. In general, marks for a certain step should be awarded if candidates’ solution indicated that the relevant concept/technique had been used.

5. In cases where a candidate answers more questions than required, the answers to all questions should be marked. However, the excess answer(s) receiving the lowest score(s) will be disregarded in the calculation of the final mark.

## Discuss the roles of centripetal and gravitational forces in maintaining orbital stability.

Centripetal forces, provided by the tension or gravitational pull, keep an object moving in a circular path, maintaining orbital stability. The tension's horizontal component provides centripetal force necessary for curved motion, while gravity's perpendicular nature relative to displacement ensures no work is done, preserving kinetic energy .

## What are the conceptual implications of Newton's third law in action-reaction force pairs in gravitational interactions?

Newton's third law states that every action has an equal and opposite reaction. In gravitational interactions, this means that while one body exerts a force on another, the second body exerts an equal force in the opposite direction. This mutual force pair doesn't result in a net force, maintaining stable interactions between celestial objects .

## How does temperature and pressure impact the volume of a balloon as it rises in altitude?

As a balloon rises in altitude, the pressure decreases faster than the temperature decreases, leading to a decrease in the balloon's volume. This is due to the greater fractional decrease in pressure compared to temperature, causing the balloon to contract despite the cooling effect .

## How does power rating and circuit configuration affect electrical energy consumption in household circuits?

Electrical energy consumption depends on the power rating and duration of appliance use. In parallel circuits, failure in one set doesn't affect others, allowing independent operation. Both elements draw rated power throughout, affecting energy use. Consumption calculations involve power ratings and duration multipliers to determine total energy use and cost .

## Evaluate the importance of unit consistency and error checking in solving physics problems involving calculations.

Unit consistency is crucial for correct physical interpretations, as errors in units can lead to incorrect magnitudes or dimensions, affecting overall accuracy. Error checking through recalculations and consistent unit application ensures correctness and reliability of solutions in physics, especially when involving multi-step calculations .

## What are the implications for forces and motion when a net fails to decelerate a falling object effectively?

When a net fails to decelerate a falling object effectively due to high velocity, the force required to decelerate the object exceeds the net's capacity to withstand it, potentially tearing the net, injuring the falling person, or preventing the firemen from holding the net tightly. These consequences illustrate the limitations of the net, the object, and the deceleration method .

## Analyze the effects of lens type on image formation and characteristics in optics.

A diverging or concave lens forms a diminished, virtual image. This result occurs because diverging lenses cause parallel rays to spread out as if they originated from a focal point on the same side of the lens as the light source, producing an upright and reduced image when the object is placed between the focus and the lens .

## Explain the interpretative challenges associated with projectile motion and displacement predictions.

Predicting projectile motion involves complexities due to varying initial conditions and external variables like air resistance. Horizontal displacement estimation is difficult as it relies on precise timing and velocity measurements, with extended falls exacerbating uncertainties. Interpretation challenges arise from balancing theoretical models against practical deviations .

## How do measurement techniques and conditions influence experimental results in physics assessments?

Measurement techniques and conditions, such as adjusting the resistance to large values initially and recording voltages with appropriate configurations, ensure experimental precision and accuracy, affecting the outcomes. Proper precautions, like opening the switch after measurements, prevent errors from equipment limitations, influencing results' validity significantly .

## Explain how gravitational forces affect the motion and energy of celestial bodies in space.

Gravitational forces act perpendicular to the motion of celestial bodies, such as the moon, preventing any work being done on them by this force and maintaining their kinetic energy constant. Additionally, according to Newton's third law, an equal and opposite gravitational force acts on another body, maintaining mutual gravitational influences without energy change through work .
