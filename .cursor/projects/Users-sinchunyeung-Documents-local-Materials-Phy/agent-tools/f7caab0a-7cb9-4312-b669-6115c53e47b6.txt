Four identical resistors and a battery are connected as shown. An electron has the greatest
energy change when it moves from
A point P to O.
B point O to N.
C point N to M.
D point M to P.
-- ans –

D
The voltage across MP (the energy source) is the highest. Therefore, an electron moves
from point M to P has the greatest energy change (gain).
If you have taken ‘energy change’ as ‘energy loss’ only and thought that the energy loss
in the battery is zero, you may think options other than D are correct. In fact, ‘energy
change’ includes ‘energy gain’.
Note that the voltages across PO and NM are the same and therefore the energy change
(loss) of an electron is the same when moving from point P to O and from point N to M.
Besides, the voltage across ON is the lowest among the four regions and therefore the
energy change of an electron is the least when moving from point O to N.
-- ans end --

voltage across XZ.
